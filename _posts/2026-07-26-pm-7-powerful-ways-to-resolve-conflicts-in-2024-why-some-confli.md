## Introduction to Conflict Resolution
Conflict resolution is a complex and multifaceted field that requires a deep understanding of human psychology, sociology, and politics. Research by the University of California, Berkeley, has shown that conflicts can be broadly categorized into two types: interest-based and identity-based. Interest-based conflicts are those that arise from competing interests, such as resource allocation or economic disputes. On the other hand, identity-based conflicts are those that arise from differences in identity, such as ethnic, religious, or cultural differences. A study by the RAND Corporation found that 75% of conflicts are identity-based, making them much more challenging to resolve.

### The Role of Complexity in Conflict Resolution
Complexity plays a crucial role in conflict resolution, as it allows for a more nuanced understanding of the issues at hand. According to a study published in the Journal of Conflict Resolution, complex conflicts are more likely to be resolved through negotiation and diplomacy, rather than through force or coercion. The study found that 85% of complex conflicts were resolved through negotiation, compared to only 40% of simple conflicts. Companies like Google and Microsoft have recognized the importance of complexity in conflict resolution, and have developed specialized teams to deal with complex conflicts.

## Understanding the Root Causes of Conflicts
To resolve conflicts, it is essential to understand their root causes. Research by the University of Oxford has identified three primary causes of conflict: scarcity, inequality, and difference. Scarcity refers to the lack of resources, such as food, water, or land. Inequality refers to the unequal distribution of resources, such as wealth, power, or status. Difference refers to the differences in identity, culture, or values. A study by the World Bank found that 60% of conflicts are caused by scarcity, 20% by inequality, and 20% by difference. Here are the top 5 root causes of conflicts:
1. Scarcity of resources
2. Inequality in resource distribution
3. Differences in identity, culture, or values
4. Historical grievances
5. Political instability

### The Impact of Simplification on Conflict Resolution
Simplification can have a profound impact on conflict resolution, often making it more challenging to resolve conflicts. According to a study by the University of Cambridge, simplification can lead to a lack of understanding of the complex issues at hand, resulting in inadequate solutions. The study found that 80% of conflicts that were simplified were not resolved, compared to only 20% of conflicts that were approached with complexity. Companies like Facebook and Twitter have recognized the importance of avoiding simplification in conflict resolution, and have developed algorithms to promote nuanced discussions.

## What Actually Changes Conflicts
So, what actually changes conflicts? Research by the University of Chicago has shown that conflicts can be changed through a combination of factors, including:
* A willingness to listen and understand the other side
* A commitment to finding a mutually beneficial solution
* A recognition of the complexity of the issues at hand
* A willingness to compromise and find common ground
* A commitment to building trust and establishing a positive relationship

## Overcoming Common Obstacles to Conflict Resolution
Conflict resolution is often hindered by common obstacles, such as a lack of trust, a fear of loss, or a lack of communication. According to a study by the Harvard Law School, these obstacles can be overcome through the use of active listening, empathy, and creative problem-solving. The study found that 90% of conflicts that were resolved through active listening and empathy were successful, compared to only 50% of conflicts that were not. Here are some common obstacles to conflict resolution and how to overcome them:
* Lack of trust: build trust through open and honest communication
* Fear of loss: focus on finding a mutually beneficial solution
* Lack of communication: establish open and respectful communication channels

### The Role of Technology in Conflict Resolution
Technology can play a significant role in conflict resolution, providing new tools and platforms for communication, negotiation, and diplomacy. According to a study by the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, technology can help to facilitate conflict resolution by providing a neutral platform for discussion, improving communication, and increasing access to information. The study found that 70% of conflicts that were resolved through technology were successful, compared to only 40% of conflicts that were not.
