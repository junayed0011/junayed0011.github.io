## What to Watch Out For
When evaluating scientific evidence, it is essential to be aware of common misconceptions and pitfalls. Some of these include:
* Confirmation bias: the tendency to seek out information that confirms our preconceptions
* Cherry-picking data: selectively presenting data that supports a particular argument
* Lack of transparency: failing to disclose methods, data, or conflicts of interest
