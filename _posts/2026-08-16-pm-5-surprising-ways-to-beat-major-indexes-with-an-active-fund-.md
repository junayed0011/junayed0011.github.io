## What to Watch Out For
While active funds like ARK Invest have shown impressive performance, there are also potential risks to consider. One of the main concerns is the high fees associated with active management. These fees can eat into returns and reduce the overall performance of the fund. Investors should also be aware of the potential for style drift, where the fund's managers deviate from their stated investment strategy. According to a report by the SEC, investors should carefully review a fund's prospectus and fee structure before investing.

### Common Misconceptions
There are several common misconceptions about active funds that investors should be aware of. One of the most common is the idea that all active funds are created equal. In reality, there is a wide range of active funds with different investment strategies and levels of risk. Investors should also be aware of the potential for over-diversification, where a fund holds too many stocks and becomes indistinguishable from an index fund. Here are some key things to consider:
1. **Fees**: Active funds often have higher fees than index funds.
2. **Risk**: Active funds can be riskier than index funds, especially if they have a high concentration of stocks in one particular industry.
3. **Style drift**: Active funds can deviate from their stated investment strategy, which can impact performance.
